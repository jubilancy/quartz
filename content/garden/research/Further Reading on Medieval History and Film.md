**Further Reading on Medieval History and Film**

Scott Manning's bibliography is the starting point here for English language materials.

- Scott Manning:  [Medievalism on Screen: An Annotated Bibliography](https://scottmanning.com/content/medievalism-on-screen-an-annotated-bibliography/)  [Internet Archive version  [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20230424203109/https://scottmanning.com/content/medievalism-on-screen-an-annotated-bibliography/)]
- Wikipedia:  [Middle Ages in Film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_Ages_in_film)  [with bibliography]
- Arthur Lindley:  [The Ahistoricism of Medieval Film](http://tlweb.latrobe.edu.au/humanities/screeningthepast/firstrelease/fir598/ALfr3a.htm)  1998 [Internet Archive version  [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20220601153011/http://tlweb.latrobe.edu.au/humanities/screeningthepast/firstrelease/fir598/ALfr3a.htm)]

- Elizabeth Lapina:  [The Middle Ages in Film Syllabus](https://history.wisc.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/202/2017/05/history600_fall2014_lapina.pdf)  2014 [Internert Archive version  [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20230118183019/https://history.wisc.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/202/2017/05/history600_fall2014_lapina.pdf)]
