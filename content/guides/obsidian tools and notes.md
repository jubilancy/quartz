# my plugins
- Audio recorder – Records audio notes and saves them as attachments.[obsidian](https://help.obsidian.md/plugins)​
    
- Backlinks – Shows links from other files to the current note, either in a separate view or at the bottom of the note.[obsidian](https://help.obsidian.md/plugins)​
    
- Bases – Creates custom views that let you edit, sort, and filter files using their properties.[obsidian](https://help.obsidian.md/plugins)​
    
- Bookmarks – Saves shortcuts to files, searches, headings, and graphs.[obsidian](https://help.obsidian.md/plugins)​
    
- Canvas – Arranges and connects notes on an infinite canvas.[obsidian](https://help.obsidian.md/plugins)​
    
- Command palette – Lets you use Cmd/Ctrl+P and start typing to invoke a command.[obsidian](https://help.obsidian.md/plugins)​
    
- Daily notes – Creates or opens today’s daily note.[obsidian](https://help.obsidian.md/plugins)​
    
- File recovery – Restores recent snapshots to recover from accidental data loss, using snapshots saved for Markdown files.[obsidian](https://help.obsidian.md/plugins)​
    
- Files – Browses the files and folders in your vault.[obsidian](https://help.obsidian.md/plugins)​
    
- Footnotes view – Shows a list of footnotes from the current note.[obsidian](https://help.obsidian.md/plugins)​
    
- Format converter – Converts Markdown from other apps to Obsidian format.[obsidian](https://help.obsidian.md/plugins)​
    
- Graph view – Visualizes the relationships between your notes.[obsidian](https://help.obsidian.md/plugins)​
    
- Note composer – Merges two notes or splits one note into two.[obsidian](https://help.obsidian.md/plugins)​
    
- Outgoing links – Shows outgoing links and unlinked mentions of other notes in the current note.[obsidian](https://help.obsidian.md/plugins)​
    
- Outline – Shows the table of contents for the current note.[obsidian](https://help.obsidian.md/plugins)​
    
- Page preview – Previews the content of an internal link while hovering.[obsidian](https://help.obsidian.md/plugins)​
    
- Properties view – Shows metadata for your files in the sidebar.[obsidian](https://help.obsidian.md/plugins)​
    
- Publish – Hosts your notes online as a website, wiki, or documentation.[obsidian](https://help.obsidian.md/plugins)​
    
- Quick switcher – Jumps to other files with your keyboard.[obsidian](https://help.obsidian.md/plugins)​
    
- Random note – Opens a random note to rediscover or review.[obsidian](https://help.obsidian.md/plugins)​
    
- Search – Searches for a keyword in all notes.[obsidian](https://help.obsidian.md/plugins)​
    
- Slash commands – Triggers commands in the editor by using the forward slash key.[obsidian](https://help.obsidian.md/plugins)​
    
- Slides – Creates a presentation using `---` to separate slides.[obsidian](https://help.obsidian.md/plugins)​
    
- Sync – Synchronizes your files through Obsidian Sync.[obsidian](https://help.obsidian.md/plugins)​
    
- Tags view – Shows a list of all tags and their number of occurrences.[obsidian](https://help.obsidian.md/plugins)​
    
- Templates – Inserts template content from a folder of template files.[obsidian](https://help.obsidian.md/plugins)​
    
- Unique note creator – Creates notes with unique timestamp prefixes for workflows like Zettelkasten or slip box.[obsidian](https://help.obsidian.md/plugins)​
    
- Web viewer – Opens external links to web pages inside Obsidian.[obsidian](https://help.obsidian.md/plugins)​
    
- Word count – Shows word count in the status bar.[obsidian](https://help.obsidian.md/plugins)​
    
- Workspaces – Saves and loads workspace layouts.[obsidian](https://help.obsidian.md/plugins)​
