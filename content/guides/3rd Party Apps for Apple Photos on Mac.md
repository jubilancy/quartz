## 3rd Party Apps for Apple Photos on Mac

**Export & management:**

- **[Mylio Photos](https://mylio.com/)** — powerful organizer, works alongside Photos
    
- **[PowerPhotos](https://fatcatsoftware.com/powerphotos/)** — merge libraries, find duplicates, better export control (~$30)
    
- **[Photosweeper](https://overmacs.com/)** — finds duplicate/similar photos ($10)
    

**Editing (as external editors inside Photos):**

- **Pixelmator Pro** — excellent Mac-native editor, integrates directly with Photos
    
- **Darkroom** — great for batch editing, free with paid upgrades
    
- **Lightroom** — industry standard if you shoot a lot
    

**For bulk export/rename specifically:**

- **Hazel** — auto-rename/move files as they're exported
    
- **Automator** (built into Mac, free) — batch rename without any install
    

For exporting 33 grad photos cleanly, the built-in Photos export with "Album Name With Number" + PNG + Most Compatible is honestly all you need.
